## 📖 About The Project

**Rent & Stay** is a hotel booking and room renting web platform developed as a **Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) Major Project**.

The platform helps users discover, compare, and book hotels or rental stays through a simple and user-friendly interface. It also enables property owners to list rooms and accommodations for travellers looking for affordable stays.

The project was designed to address common challenges faced by travellers such as:

- Limited accommodation options
- High booking costs
- Lack of transparency
- Complex booking processes
- Difficulty comparing locations and facilities

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

✅ Simplify room and hotel search
